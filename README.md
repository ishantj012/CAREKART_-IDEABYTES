# 🏥 CareKart — Smart Healthcare Platform

**CareKart** is an AI-powered healthcare platform designed to bring essential healthcare services together in one place.

### 💡 What CareKart Offers

* 🤖 **AI Healthcare Assistant** – Smart health guidance and assistance
* 👨‍⚕️ **Doctor Discovery** – Find doctors by specialization and availability
* 📅 **Appointment Booking** – Book online or in-person consultations
* 💊 **Pharmacy** – Search and order medicines
* 🧪 **Medical Tests** – Discover and book diagnostic tests
* 📹 **Video Consultation** – Connect with doctors remotely
* 📊 **Personalized Recommendations** – AI-based healthcare suggestions

### 🛠️ Technology

* **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript
* **Backend:** Java, Spring Boot
* **Database:** PostgreSQL
* **AI:** AI-powered assistance & recommendations
* **Deployment:** Vercel / Replit

### 🎯 Goal

CareKart aims to make healthcare **simpler, smarter, more accessible, and more convenient** by connecting patients with doctors, pharmacies, diagnostic services, and AI assistance through a single platform.

### 🚀 Future Scope

* AI-powered health analysis
* Digital health records
* Wearable integration
* Medicine reminders
* Emergency assistance
* Multilingual AI healthcare assistant
